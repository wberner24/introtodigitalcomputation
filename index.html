Introduction to
Digital Computation

A First-Principles Approach
By Wade Berner · Southern Illinois University Edwardsville · 2025

Everything is a computer. Okay, not literally everything — but far more than you might suspect. This book is for anyone who wants to understand how digital computers actually work: not just how to use them, but what's happening underneath. No prior experience in computer science or engineering is required. All you need is curiosity and a willingness to think logically.

We'll build your understanding one layer at a time, from the physical meaning of a 1 and a 0 all the way up to cloud infrastructure and system administration. Every concept connects to the one before it.

Unit 1 — Foundations
01
Binary
What digital means. Why computers use base-2. Counting, converting, and thinking in binary.
02
Physical Bits
How 1s and 0s exist in the real world — transistors, electrical signals, optical storage, and magnetic media.
03
Data Representation
From bits to bytes to everything else — how computers encode text, images, audio, and video.
Unit 2 — Hardware
04
Processors
The CPU: how it works, what the specs mean, and how to evaluate them as an IT professional.
05
Memory & Storage
The full storage hierarchy — RAM, cache, SSD, HDD — and the latency, cost, and capacity tradeoffs between them.
06
I/O & System Architecture
Motherboards, buses, ports, and peripherals. How the components of a computer connect and communicate.
Unit 3 — Software
07
System vs. Application Software
Firmware, drivers, operating systems, middleware, and applications — the software stack explained.
08
Operating Systems
What an OS actually does, and a practical comparison of Windows, macOS, and Linux for IT decision-making.
09
File Systems & Directory Services
NTFS, ext4, permissions, fault tolerance, journaling, and Active Directory.
Unit 4 — Networks
10
Networking Fundamentals
Bits on a wire to packets to protocols. The OSI model as a thinking tool. IP, DNS, wired and wireless.
11
Network Architecture & Communication
LANs, WANs, VPNs, switches, routers, firewalls, and how a web request actually works end-to-end.
Unit 5 — Administration & Modern Infrastructure
12
System Administration Fundamentals
User management, patching, monitoring, backups, and an introduction to CLI and scripting.
13
Virtualization & Cloud Computing
Hypervisors, VMs, containers, IaaS/PaaS/SaaS, and the business case for cloud.
14
Putting It All Together
A top-to-bottom walkthrough of a real IT scenario, touching every layer from binary to cloud.
